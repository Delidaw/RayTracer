How the page is connected

      
One interface, two execution paths

    

    
The WebGL preview executes on the laptop GPU and redraws only when necessary. The Python button sends mass, spin, camera distance, field of view, resolution and step count to /render. Flask invokes your existing renderer, saves the scientific output, and returns its image path to this page.
