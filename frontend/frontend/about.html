The Idea

General Relativity tells us that mass and energy curve spacetime, while matter and light follow the resulting geometry.

Photon Forge solves these equations numerically so that geodesics can be studied and visualized rather than remaining only abstract mathematics.

⚙

The Engine

Built as a modular computational physics engine, Photon Forge separates spacetime models, geodesic integration, rendering and validation.

  • Einstein field-equation framework
  • Schwarzschild and Kerr metrics
  • Christoffel-symbol calculation
  • Null and particle geodesics
  • RK4 numerical integration
  • Photon ray tracing
  • Accretion-disk interaction
  • Validation and testing
◌

The Purpose

The goal is to make the physics of black holes more accessible by connecting mathematical equations to observable structures and interactive simulations.

Photon Forge is designed for students, researchers and anyone curious about how General Relativity shapes the universe.

∿

Our Approach

We combine physics-first modelling with modern computation and high-performance visualization.

The interactive GPU renderer provides immediate exploration, while the Python backend acts as the numerical reference implementation.
